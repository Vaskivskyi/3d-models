# Filament spool insert

The model is made to be inserted into the filament spool with e.g. desiccant and / or sensors to monitor the humidity.

## Printing

Prints well with:

- 0.2 mm / 0.2 mm layer height
- 150 mm/s
- no supports, no rafts, no brims

Printed on:

- [Anycubic Kobra 2 Pro](https://amzn.to/4e0Dz7R)

Printed with:

- [AzureFilm Refill PLA filament](https://azurefilm.com/product/refill-pla-filament-green/?attribute_pa_filament-diameter=1-75mm&attribute_pa_filament-weight=1000g)

## Compatible

Most of the spools with the central hole equal or larger than `54 mm` in diameter. Designed for eSUN cardboard spools to fit tightly.
